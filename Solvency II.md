## Solvency I

Solvency I was the preceding EU framework.

Capital requirements relied largely on business-volume measures:

- Non-life: premiums and claims
- Life: mathematical reserves and sums at risk

It was simpler but less sensitive to investment risks, guarantees, and assetâ€“liability interactions.

Solvency II introduced a broader economic and risk-based approach.
