# Mathematics Refresher

Start with [[Probability]] for expected value, variance, and quantiles. Then use [[Probability Distributions]] to see a few common models. For the complete applied route, follow [[Actuarial Refresher - 10 Hours]].

## Core reading path

1. [[Probability]] and [[Probability Distributions]] — Random variables, moments, and common distributions.
2. [[Time Value of Money]] — Interest, discount factors, cash flows, and yield curves.
3. [[Statistics]], [[Statistical Estimation]], and [[Correlation and Dependence]] — Learn assumptions from data and combine risks.
4. [[Survival Models and Life Tables]] — Turn mortality into contingent cash flows.
5. [[Stochastic Processes]] and [[Stochastic Calculus]] — Model changes through time.
6. [[Monte Carlo Simulation]] — Estimate quantities that are difficult to calculate directly.
7. [[Economic Scenario Generator]] — Apply the mathematics to insurance valuation and risk.

## Applied routes

- [[Insurance Types]] — Choose life, motor, or health business and its mathematics.
- [[Risk Measures and Diversification]] — Move from expected results to adverse outcomes and capital.
- [[Insurance Pricing and GLMs]] and [[Claims Reserving]] — Explore non-life pricing and liabilities.
