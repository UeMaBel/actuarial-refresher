# Solvency II - Pillar III - Reporting and Disclosure

> Give supervisors and the public structured information about the insurer’s risks, financial condition, and capital position.


## Supervisory reporting

Insurers provide quantitative and qualitative information to their supervisor.

This includes:

- **QRTs — Quantitative Reporting Templates:** Structured information on assets, liabilities, own funds, capital requirements, and other relevant exposures.
- **RSR — Regular Supervisory Report:** Detailed qualitative information for supervisory assessment.

Reporting frequency and scope depend on the applicable requirements and exemptions.

## Public disclosure

**SFCR — Solvency and Financial Condition Report**

The public report explains areas such as:

- Business and performance
- System of governance
- Risk profile
- Valuation for solvency purposes
- Capital management

It helps readers understand the insurer’s solvency position and the assumptions behind it.

## Where the reported information comes from

Pillar I supplies economic valuations, own funds, and capital calculations.

Pillar II supplies information about governance, risk management, and the insurer’s assessment processes.

Pillar III communicates these results; it does not create a separate valuation framework.

## Relationship to IFRS reporting

IFRS financial statements and Solvency II reports serve different purposes.

Their asset, liability, and equity figures can differ because their measurement rules differ.

A clear explanation of these differences helps readers interpret the insurer’s financial position.
